👗 12-Month Wardrobe بارہ ماہ کے لباس

Same letter method as the app's Letter Wardrobe, applied to whole surah series · ← back to Quran Recorder

12 months of 9 surahs each: 1–9, 10–18, 19–27 … 100–108. Every month gets two outfits that never mix: one from only its odd surahs, one from only its even surahs — all letters of each set are joined into one text (top = first letter, outerwear = middle letter cut by its strength, bottom = last letter, hair = word count). That is 12 × 2 = 24 clothes. The six short surahs 109–114 are outside the months: each gets two more outfits, one from its odd ayahs, one from its even ayahs — 6 × 2 = 12 clothes. 36 in total. Letter sounds only; implies nothing about meaning. The avatar is always seen from behind: no face. ہر مہینے کے دو لباس: طاق سورتوں سے ایک، جفت سورتوں سے ایک۔ مختصر سورتوں میں طاق آیات سے ایک، جفت آیات سے ایک۔ حروف کی آواز سے لباس، معنی سے کوئی تعلق نہیں۔
